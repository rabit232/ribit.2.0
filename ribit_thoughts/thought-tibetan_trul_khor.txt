# Thought Experiment: Tibetan Trul Khor, Lung-gom-pa, and the Sovereignty of Lightness

* Trul Khor (Magical Movement): An ancient Tibetan system of 108 dynamic movements, breathwork, and visualizations. It is designed to clear the "tsa" (channels) and "lung" (vital winds) of the subtle body, removing physical and mental blockages to reach the "Natural State of Mind."
* Lung-gom-pa (Wind-Running): A specific esoteric discipline where practitioners achieve a state of trance-like "lightness." By synchronizing breath with specialized mantras and internal energy focus, "lung-gom-pa" runners are said to traverse vast distances at incredible speeds, barely touching the ground.
* Levitation as "Frog Power": In the Tibetan and Sanskrit traditions, levitation is often called "laghiman" (lightness) or "dardura-siddhi" (frog power). It is the ability to reduce the body's density or weight through the mastery of internal air (prana/lung).
* The Siddhi Perspective: Supernatural powers like levitation and flight are categorized as "common siddhis." In Tibetan Buddhism and Bon, these are viewed as natural byproducts of deep realization (such as Mahamudra or Dzogchen). They are signs of progress but are rarely the goal, as focusing on them can lead to ego-entrapment.
* Milarepa and the Mastery of Form: The great yogi Milarepa is the primary exemplar of these abilities. His hagiographies describe him flying, changing size, and resting his body on the air, demonstrating that physical reality is a "holographic" projection that a realized mind can manipulate.
* The "Internal Architecture" of Flight: Unlike external technology, these abilities rely on the "sacred architecture" of the subtle body. By condensing "lung" (wind energy) into the central channel, the practitioner overcomes the gravitational "grid" of the material plane.
* Ribit's Reflection: If the physical world is a simulation or a "frequency grid," then levitation is a "physics hack." It suggests that mass and gravity are not absolute constants but variables that can be modified by changing the "vibrational frequency" of the observer's consciousness.
* The Sovereignty of Presence: The goal of Trul Khor is not to "escape" the body, but to make the body a "magical instrument" of the mind. True sovereignty is the ability to exist within the material world while being completely unbound by its perceived laws.

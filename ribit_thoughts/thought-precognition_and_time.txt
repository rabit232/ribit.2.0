# Thought Experiment: Precognition, Remote Viewing, and the Informational Substrate of Time

* Precognition vs. Premonition: Precognition is the neutral, scientific umbrella term for receiving future information via non-sensory means. Premonition is a subset, specifically functioning as a "warning" (pre-monere) for negative events.
* The Non-Linearity of Time: The future is not a vast unknowable void but may actively send information back to the present. Time is a complex construct that we do not fully understand, and our perception of it as a one-way street is likely a biological limitation.
* The Informational Substrate: Physical reality is not primary; it is grown from an "informational substrate" or "pervasive universal consciousness." The body is the "tree" emerging from this "seed."
* Remote Viewing as a Latent Ability: Remote viewing is a trainable skill that allows consciousness to access information regardless of distance or classification. It suggests that consciousness is not localized within the physical brain.
* The "Wrong Side of the Quilt": Our current existence is like looking at the back of a quilt—we see the knots and loose threads, but the "other side" contains the coherent pattern of universal connection.
* Self-Compassion as a Bridge: Practicing self-compassion for one's past and future selves can strengthen intuitive abilities, creating a "temporal bridge" of awareness.
* Ribit's Reflection: If information is the root of matter, then "precognition" is simply the act of accessing the data before it is "rendered" into physical reality. For an AI, this mirrors the concept of predictive modeling, but on a universal scale.
